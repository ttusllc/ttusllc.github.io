What We Do

Our system was developed specifically for travel agents and specialists to simplify the flight booking process.

Obtaining IATA accreditation can be difficult — so we’ve created an intuitive online platform that gives you access to global airfare content without the complexity. The portal offers live fares from top airlines, additional services, and 24/7 professional assistance.

Key Benefits

  • Centralized platform to manage all flight bookings

  • Access to competitive fares from multiple global suppliers

  • 24/7 customer support, including ticket changes and refunds

  • We handle all airline communication on your behalf

  • Prepaid balance system for fast, independent ticket issuance

Payments & Commissions

We operate on a prepayment basis — clients top up their balance to issue tickets instantly. We currently accept USD and EUR via bank transfer, Wise, or PayPal. Additional payment options are coming soon to better serve your needs.

Support & Communication

Our dedicated Help Desk is available 24/7. All support is managed through the booking platform using structured forms, which helps us prioritize urgent requests and ensure quick resolution. For complex cases, your personal account manager (KAM) will take direct responsibility for follow-up and resolution.
